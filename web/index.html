About Dam Storage Metrics

Live Storage (Useful Content): The active volume of water above the lowest outlet / silt level available for municipal drinking, agricultural irrigation, and hydroelectric generation.

Dead Storage: Water stored below the lowest sluice gates that cannot be drained by gravity, reserved for silt accumulation.

Gross Storage: The entire volume of water stored in the reservoir (Live Storage + Dead Storage).

Hydrological Units Conversion

  • 1 MCM (Million Cubic Metres) = 1,000 Million Litres = 100 Crore Litres
  • 1 TMC (Thousand Million Cubic Feet) = 28.317 MCM = 2,831.7 Crore Litres
  • 1 MCM = 0.035315 TMC

Data Source: Maharashtra Water Resources Department (WRD) Daily Storage Bulletins, published daily at 08:00 AM IST.

From a Course Project to a Public Platform

The journey from a university DBMS assignment to a statewide civic resource

This website originally began as an academic project for the Database Management System (DBMS) course during undergraduate studies in Instrumentation and Control Engineering at Vishwakarma Institute of Technology (VIT), Pune.

The initial goal was to ground theoretical relational database concepts—such as schema normalisation (3NF), relational views, transaction processing, and referential integrity—in a genuine, real-world public data problem rather than a simulated classroom assignment.

Recognising that official water resources bulletins were distributed across daily PDF reports that made historical tracking and regional comparison difficult, the project expanded far beyond course requirements. It evolved into a complete, automated, statewide information platform that archives daily storage records and presents them openly for citizens, researchers, students, and civic communities.

1

Academic Idea

Applying fundamental DBMS concepts to real-world government water storage data.

2

Database Project

Designing a normalised relational database schema (3NF) to store dam water-storage records.

3

Automated Data Platform

Building automated scraping and scheduled pipelines to maintain continuous daily records.

4

Public Dashboard

Creating an interactive, accessible dashboard for citizens, students, and researchers.
